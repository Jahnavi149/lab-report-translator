# Scope

[← Back to README](../README.md)

What the app is for, and the few things a person should still check.

## What it is

An explainer for people who cannot read their own lab report. It repeats the printed values, says inside or outside the printed range, and suggests questions to ask a doctor. It is **not** a medical device, is not clinically validated, and never diagnoses or advises.

## Worth knowing

- **Photos:** a name printed with no label on a photo cannot be found, so the upload screen offers a crop step to remove the header. Handwriting, signatures, logos, faces and QR codes are not painted out. PDFs are unaffected.
- **Scans:** the app reads digital PDFs. A scanned PDF should be uploaded as a photo.
- **Unusual layouts:** a line that cannot be read safely is skipped and listed, never guessed.
- **Native-speaker review:** the interface text, translations and voice word lists for the non-English languages were written without a native-speaker review. A word the voice lists miss costs one fixed reply, never a wrong answer.
- **Voice:** tested end to end in a browser with synthetic speech and through the real transcriber for most languages, not yet with real people.
